## Market Overview  
The cloud security landscape is evolving rapidly as enterprises shift workloads to hybrid and multi‑cloud environments. Organizations now require scalable, automated security controls that can dynamically adapt to changing workloads, enforce least‑privilege access, and ensure continuous compliance across disparate regulatory regimes. This demand is driving significant investment in AI‑enhanced threat detection and Zero‑Trust architectures, especially among mid‑market firms that lack the resources for extensive in‑house security teams.  

Simultaneously, the complexity of integrating multiple point solutions and the high cost of comprehensive security platforms have limited broad adoption. Vendors are responding with subscription‑based SaaS models that bundle detection, response, and compliance capabilities, aiming to lower entry barriers and provide predictable pricing. As regulatory frameworks become more fragmented globally, the need for unified, policy‑driven security that can be managed centrally is increasingly critical.
